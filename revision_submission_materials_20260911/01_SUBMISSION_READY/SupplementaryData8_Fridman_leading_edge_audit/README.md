# Supplementary Data 8: Fridman senescence-signature leading-edge audit

This supplementary data supports the Results statement describing the heterogeneous behavior of `FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_UP` and `FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_DN` across species and time points in Figure 6.

The files report the frozen NES, nominal P value, panel-level BH-FDR, represented gene-set size and leading-edge members for mouse 1 week, mouse 4 weeks, early pig 1 week, early pig 4 weeks and chronic pig 52 weeks. The long-format member table lists each leading-edge gene separately and identifies the representative genes discussed in the manuscript.

Neither Fridman signature passed the eight-set panel-level FDR correction. The annotations identifying ECM/senescence-associated genes, early-pig negative-NES drivers and the small-overlap FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_DN representatives are descriptive labels for interpretation, not additional statistical tests. The source rows were extracted from the frozen Figure 6 result table; no enrichment statistics were recomputed.
