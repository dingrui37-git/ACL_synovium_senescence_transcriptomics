# Supplementary Data 7: Figure 6 p53 leading-edge audit

This file supports the Figure 6 Results statement describing the negative HALLMARK_P53_PATHWAY NES in early pig synovium.

The table contains the complete fgsea leading-edge members for the early-pig 1-week and 4-week p53 contrasts, together with five prespecified canonical stress/checkpoint genes (TP53, BAX, GADD45A, GADD45B and CDKN1A). For each gene, the table reports logFC, the ranked-statistic value used for GSEA, nominal P value, differential-expression FDR and the corresponding p53 NES and panel FDR.

The negative-NES leading-edge members were negatively ranked, whereas the selected canonical stress/checkpoint genes were positively ranked at early pig 1 week. This audit supports describing the pattern as mixed and time-dependent rather than as complete suppression of p53-related transcription. The p53 gene-set enrichment itself did not pass panel-level FDR correction.

The leading-edge members are reproduced from the frozen Figure 6 eight-set GSEA result. The gene-level statistics are joined from the corresponding frozen early-pig ranked-transcriptome input files.
