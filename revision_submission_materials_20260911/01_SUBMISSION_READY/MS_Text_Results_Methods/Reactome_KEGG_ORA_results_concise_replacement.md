# Concise Results replacement: Reactome/KEGG broader pathway analysis

The direction-consistent persistent response comprised 1,416 mouse genes, including 970 persistently upregulated and 446 persistently downregulated genes. Direction-stratified Reactome and KEGG over-representation analysis identified 69 FDR-supported pathway records for the persistent-up list and 32 for the persistent-down list [Supplementary Fig. X; Supplementary Table X].

Persistent-up genes were strongly concentrated in extracellular-matrix synthesis and turnover. Reactome extracellular matrix organization included 89 of 225 pathway genes (enrichment ratio = 5.76, FDR = 1.13 × 10−43), collagen formation included 36 of 71 genes (enrichment ratio = 7.39, FDR = 5.93 × 10−21), and extracellular-matrix degradation included 38 of 81 genes (enrichment ratio = 6.83, FDR = 8.07 × 10−21). Concordant KEGG enrichments included ECM–receptor interaction (26/80 genes; enrichment ratio = 4.41; FDR = 3.25 × 10−9), focal adhesion (32/191; enrichment ratio = 2.27; FDR = 3.43 × 10−4) and cytokine–cytokine receptor interaction (32/173; enrichment ratio = 2.51; FDR = 4.71 × 10−5). Reactome neutrophil degranulation and hemostasis were also supported (FDR = 0.026 and 0.030, respectively).

Persistent-down genes were enriched for metabolic and cytoprotective programs. KEGG PPAR signaling included 19 of 62 genes (enrichment ratio = 7.72, FDR = 1.47 × 10−9), and AMPK signaling included 18 of 115 genes (enrichment ratio = 3.94, FDR = 1.28 × 10−4). Reactome metabolism of lipids included 41 of 496 genes (enrichment ratio = 2.60, FDR = 6.21 × 10−6), while cytoprotection by HMOX1 included 9 of 43 genes (enrichment ratio = 6.58, FDR = 1.01 × 10−3). Most persistent effects were quantitatively attenuated at 4 weeks: only 205 of 970 persistent-up genes and 43 of 446 persistent-down genes showed a larger absolute effect at 4 weeks than at 1 week. Together, these analyses identified coordinated matrix remodeling, adhesion and inflammatory signaling among persistent-up genes, alongside reduced metabolic and cytoprotective programs among persistent-down genes. These enrichments describe molecular processes represented among the persistent genes and do not establish pathway-level causality [Supplementary Fig. X; Supplementary Table X].
