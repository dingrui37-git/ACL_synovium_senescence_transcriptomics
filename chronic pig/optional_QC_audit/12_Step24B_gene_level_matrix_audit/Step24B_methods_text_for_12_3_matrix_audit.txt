After the 96 processed Salmon quant.sf files passed the separate quantification-level integrity QC, tximport-derived gene-level estimated counts, TPM abundance and effective-length matrices were audited for downstream use. The audit confirmed matrix dimensions, gene_id ordering, sample-column consistency across the three tximport-derived matrices, sample-level total estimated counts, detected-gene counts, gene-level TPM totals and effective-length validity. Matrix sample identifiers were additionally checked for tissue and treatment balance, and optional matching to the Step23 quant-file inventory was recorded. This clean audit focuses on tximport gene-level matrix consistency and does not repeat the full per-file Salmon quant.sf integrity QC.
