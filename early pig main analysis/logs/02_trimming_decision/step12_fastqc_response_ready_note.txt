Pig early raw-read QC interpretation note (response-ready draft):

To address the concern that the original workflow lacked raw-read-level QC evidence, we performed FastQC on all 36 FASTQ files corresponding to the 18 retained pig early core samples. 
The key trimming-related modules showed uniformly acceptable results: Adapter Content = PASS in 36/36 files, Per base sequence quality = PASS in 36/36 files, and Per sequence quality scores = PASS in 36/36 files. 
Therefore, the FastQC results did not support widespread adapter contamination or systematic low-quality tailing as major issues requiring mandatory preprocessing trimming. 
Observed warnings/failures in modules such as Per base sequence content and Sequence Duplication Levels were interpreted with caution, as these modules are often influenced by RNA-seq library composition and expression structure and are not, by themselves, sufficient evidence that trimming is necessary. 
On this basis, the pig early analysis was carried forward without an additional trimming step, and the full raw-read QC evidence was retained in the rebuilt submission structure.
