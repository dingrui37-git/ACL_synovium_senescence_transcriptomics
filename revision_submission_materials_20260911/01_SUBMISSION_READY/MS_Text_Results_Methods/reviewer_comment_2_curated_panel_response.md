# Reviewer #2 — additional senescence-related pathway analysis

## Comment

“Senescence-associated synovial remodeling is the central theme of the manuscript; however, the pathway-level interpretation is restricted to two prespecified Hallmark gene sets: HALLMARK_INFLAMMATORY_RESPONSE and HALLMARK_EPITHELIAL_MESENCHYMAL_TRANSITION. Please justify the selection of only these two readouts. Given that MSigDB contains additional senescence-related gene sets and pathways, the authors should consider incorporating these into the enrichment analyses. Doing so would provide more direct support for the manuscript’s senescence-related claims and offer a more comprehensive biological interpretation of the findings.”

## Response

We thank the reviewer for this important suggestion. We agree that inflammation and epithelial–mesenchymal transition alone do not directly establish a senescence-related transcriptional state. For the revision, we therefore replaced the previous broad keyword-derived list with a focused, biologically structured eight-signature senescence panel. This design separates direct senescence/SASP evidence from supporting damage- and cell-cycle-related context, while avoiding a large, heterogeneous collection of loosely related pathway names.

The primary direct-evidence tier included REACTOME_CELLULAR_SENESCENCE, REACTOME_SENESCENCE_ASSOCIATED_SECRETORY_PHENOTYPE_SASP, FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_UP, FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_DN, and SenMayo. SenMayo was obtained from the original Supplementary Data 1 of Saul et al.; the authors’ murine list was used directly for mouse, and the published human list was mapped to pig using g:Profiler, with the complete mapping retained in the Supplementary material. The supporting mechanistic tier included HALLMARK_P53_PATHWAY, HALLMARK_E2F_TARGETS, and HALLMARK_G2M_CHECKPOINT. We explicitly interpret this latter tier as mechanistic context rather than as stand-alone evidence of senescence.

We repeated preranked GSEA using the same five frozen ranked transcriptome vectors used for the original Figure 3B-style analyses: mouse 1W and 4W, pig early 1W and 4W, and pig chronic 52W. Genes were ranked with the predefined statistic sign(logFC) × −log10(P value used for ranking). The primary multiplicity family was all eight prespecified entries within each comparison; nominal GSEA p values were adjusted by Benjamini–Hochberg within that comparison. A uniform minimum overlap of five genes was used because FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_DN contains only 10 source genes and had 8–9 genes represented in each ranked vector; this preserves the complete eight-entry panel and its correction family. The complete testability audit is provided in the Supplementary material.

The additional analysis supports a time- and species-dependent, rather than uniform, senescence-associated remodeling pattern. In mouse, SenMayo was positively enriched at both 1W (NES = 2.22, FDR = 5.46 × 10^-8) and 4W (NES = 2.07, FDR = 2.27 × 10^-6). In pig, REACTOME_CELLULAR_SENESCENCE was positively enriched at early 1W (NES = 1.82, FDR = 3.31 × 10^-5), early 4W (NES = 1.43, FDR = 0.0176), and chronic 52W (NES = 1.50, FDR = 0.0202). REACTOME_SENESCENCE_ASSOCIATED_SECRETORY_PHENOTYPE_SASP was positively enriched at pig early 1W (NES = 1.51, FDR = 0.0231) and chronic 52W (NES = 1.59, FDR = 0.0202). Neither Fridman signature passed panel FDR correction. In particular, FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_DN showed positive NES values in all contrasts, which is directionally discordant with enrichment of a gene set defined as down-regulated in senescence; the corresponding leading-edge audit is provided in Supplementary Data 8.

The three supporting Hallmark readouts further constrain interpretation. HALLMARK_P53_PATHWAY was not significant after panel FDR correction. HALLMARK_E2F_TARGETS and HALLMARK_G2M_CHECKPOINT were positively enriched, rather than suppressed, in several acute contrasts; these changes are more consistent with injury-associated proliferative or compositional activity than with a universal cell-cycle-arrest program. We have therefore revised the text to avoid treating these supportive pathways as direct proof of senescence.

Together, these results provide direct but heterogeneous support for senescence/SASP-associated synovial remodeling: persistent SenMayo enrichment in mouse, and persistent Reactome cellular-senescence enrichment with temporally restricted SASP enrichment in pig. This complements, rather than replaces, the cross-species direction-consistent gene analysis.

## Proposed Methods text

To provide a focused assessment of senescence-related pathway activity, we performed preranked GSEA on the five prespecified ranked transcriptome contrasts using an eight-set panel. The direct senescence/SASP tier comprised REACTOME_CELLULAR_SENESCENCE, REACTOME_SENESCENCE_ASSOCIATED_SECRETORY_PHENOTYPE_SASP, FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_UP, FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_DN, and SenMayo. The supporting mechanistic tier comprised HALLMARK_P53_PATHWAY, HALLMARK_E2F_TARGETS, and HALLMARK_G2M_CHECKPOINT; these readouts were not interpreted as stand-alone evidence of senescence. Genes were ranked with sign(logFC) × −log10(P value used for ranking) from the frozen rank files. Reactome, Fridman and Hallmark gene sets were obtained through msigdbr for the ranked species using its species-specific human-MSigDB orthologue projection, and duplicate symbols were removed. SenMayo used the authors’ original murine list directly for mouse; the original human list was mapped to Sus scrofa using g:Profiler for pig, retaining unique mapped pig symbols and the complete mapping audit. GSEA used fgsea with a minimum represented gene-set size of five, maximum size of 500, and exact multilevel p-value calculation. The minimum was reduced from the original Hallmark threshold of 10 to retain FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_DN, which contains only 10 source genes and 8–9 represented genes per ranked vector. For each comparison, all eight panel entries constituted one prespecified multiplicity family and nominal p values were adjusted by Benjamini–Hochberg. For FRIDMAN_SENESCENCE_DN, a negative NES was considered directionally concordant because this gene set represents genes down-regulated in senescence. P53, E2F, and G2/M results were interpreted as mechanistic context and not as independent diagnostic evidence of senescence.

## Proposed Results text

A focused eight-signature senescence panel revealed heterogeneous but persistent senescence-associated remodeling across species and time. In mouse synovium, SenMayo was positively enriched at both 1W (NES = 2.22, FDR = 5.46 × 10^-8) and 4W (NES = 2.07, FDR = 2.27 × 10^-6), whereas the Reactome cellular-senescence and SASP signatures were not FDR significant. In pig synovium, REACTOME_CELLULAR_SENESCENCE was positively enriched at early 1W (NES = 1.82, FDR = 3.31 × 10^-5), early 4W (NES = 1.43, FDR = 0.0176), and chronic 52W (NES = 1.50, FDR = 0.0202). REACTOME_SENESCENCE_ASSOCIATED_SECRETORY_PHENOTYPE_SASP was positively enriched at early 1W (NES = 1.51, FDR = 0.0231) and chronic 52W (NES = 1.59, FDR = 0.0202). The Fridman up- and down-regulated senescence signatures did not meet panel FDR significance. P53 pathway enrichment was not FDR significant, while positive E2F/G2M enrichment in several contrasts did not support a uniform cell-cycle-arrest interpretation. Thus, the panel supports a species- and time-dependent senescence/SASP-associated transcriptional program rather than a single invariant senescence signature.

## Figure 6 integration and FDR-scope clarification

For Figure 6, the eight entries above remain the new senescence-focused testing family. The pre-existing HALLMARK_INFLAMMATORY_RESPONSE and HALLMARK_EPITHELIAL_MESENCHYMAL_TRANSITION readouts are displayed for biological context but retain the adjusted P values from the original complete 50-Hallmark analyses; they are not re-adjusted together with the eight new entries. The directional-score row is separate from both pathway families. The mouse score is a descriptive derivation-species reference calculated from the 75 mouse genes corresponding to the measurable one-to-one pig orthologues and their prespecified directions. Because these genes and directions were derived from the mouse data, mouse score P values are not interpreted as independent validation evidence and no inferential FDR is assigned to mouse cells. The pig score is the projected 75-orthologue validation score tested with the prespecified rank-sum comparisons, while retaining the original dataset-specific primary score-analysis BH-FDR families, comprising 12 score-comparison rows for early pig and six score definitions for chronic pig. This distinction is now recorded in the Figure 6 source data, legend contract and manuscript Methods.
